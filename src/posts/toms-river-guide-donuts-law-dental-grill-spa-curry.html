Toms River is the largest town in Ocean County, and its listings in the directory cover a lot of ground. This guide picks six of them: a donut stand, a law firm, a dental office, a grill, a day spa and a Punjabi restaurant.

It is not a ranking. Each section sticks to what the business says on its own website and what the directory lists, so you can decide which ones are worth a call. You can also browse everything in Toms River.

Ob-Co's Donuts, Toms River

Ob-Co's Donuts is at 547 Fischer Blvd. Its website says, "We have been serving the Jersey Shore area since 1953," and that customers order from the outside, at a window beneath an awning. The directory describes it as a walk-up stand making donuts and crumb cakes fresh daily.

The site's donut list includes Cinnamon Apple Cider, Bavarian Cream and Boston Cream, with Apple Fritter and Cheese Pockets among the specials. The directory also lists platters, coffee and seasonal flavors. No hours are listed in the directory, so call (732) 270-3882 before you go.

Brady & Kunz, Toms River

Brady & Kunz is a law firm in the Bayview Corporate Center at 1433 Hooper Avenue, Suite 310. Its website names two attorneys, Thomas E. Kunz and Terry F. Brady, and focuses on workers' compensation and Social Security disability cases.

The site also lists personal injury, real estate, criminal defense, family law, land use and planning, and wills and estates. It offers a free consultation, booked through its contact page or by phone at (732) 240-4600. The website says its workers' compensation attorneys are "Certified" through a New Jersey Supreme Court designation. We have not verified that, so ask the firm directly about it.

Elite Dental Center of Toms River, Toms River

Elite Dental is a family practice at 1314 Hooper Avenue. Its website lists two dentists, Dr. George Lambrinos and Dr. Abe Saff, and says the office offers "a complete range of cosmetic dentistry and restorative solutions."

The services list is long: checkups, crowns and bridges, dental implants, Invisalign, Lumineers, teeth whitening, root canal treatment, laser dentistry and kid-friendly dentistry. The site also says its emergency dentist services are ready to assist with urgent needs. The directory lists no hours for this office, so call (732) 286-7600 to ask about appointments and insurance.

Royal Grill, Toms River

Royal Grill is on Route 37 East, at number 10. Its menu centers on gyros, kebabs and platters, and the website promotes new French fry bowls. It also says "WE ARE 100% HALAL" and invites visitors with dietary concerns to get in touch.

The site has an online ordering button and a contact form for catering and special requests. The directory lists hours of 10am to 10pm Monday through Saturday, with no Sunday hours listed. Hours can change, so call (732) 505-9696 first.

Spa Virtue, Toms River

Spa Virtue is a day spa at 1922 Hooper Ave, Suite 2D. Its website describes Cryoskin as a non-invasive treatment using heat and cold therapy, and a Hydrafacial as a three-step facial that cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates. The menu also includes Slim Wrap body wraps, massage and lash lifts.

According to the site, infrared sauna sessions are offered as part of customized packages, and you can reserve online or call (732) 323-3000. The directory lists these hours; call ahead to confirm.

Ellie's Indian Curry Corner, Toms River

Ellie's is a Punjabi restaurant in Suite 4 at 73 Main St, on the upper level. Its website describes it as family owned, with food made from scratch every day, and says it is BYOB. It also has a second location in Belmar.

The online menu includes Butter Chicken, Lamb Seekh Kabab, Palak Paneer, Dal Makhani and Goat Dum Biryani. The site also lists lunch buffets at the Toms River location on Sundays and Thursdays, by reservation, plus pickup, delivery, private events and catering. The directory lists hours of 11am to 9:30pm Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday, and 11am to 10pm Friday and Saturday, with no Tuesday hours listed. Call (848) 367-3922 to confirm hours and buffet times.

Using this guide

These six sit mostly along Hooper Avenue, Route 37 and Main Street, so a few could fit into one trip. Hours, menus and prices change, and several listings here have no hours at all, so a quick call saves a wasted drive.
