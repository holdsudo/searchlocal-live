Gregory's Seafood Market & Restaurant is at 2064 Route 37 in Manchester. The name tells you the setup: it is a retail fish market and a restaurant at one address. This article walks through how the business describes each side on its own website.

This is a profile of one business, not a ranking. Everything below comes from gregorys-seafood.com as we read it on October 26, 2026. The site does not list menu prices or a founding date, so this article does not either.

The market side

The website describes the market as a retail fish market with daily specials. Its navigation has a specials page and a link to weekend specials, so the offerings can change from day to day. The business also says its Facebook followers may get special offers and promotions on the seafood menu and the fresh fish market.

The website says the fresh fish is sold without preservatives or chemicals. That is the business's own statement, and we have not verified it. If you are buying fish to cook at home, the counter is the place to ask what came in that day and how it was handled.

The restaurant side

The restaurant is a dine-in operation. The website says every dish is made to order and that the kitchen uses fresh local produce. The site has a menu page, though the page we read did not list specific dishes on the front page, so check the menu directly before you go.

The website also advertises summer patio seating. It does not give dates or hours for the patio, so call before planning an outdoor meal.

Gift cards and the email list

The website says gift cards are available and can be purchased at the counter only. If you are planning to give one, that means a trip in person rather than an online order.

The site also describes an email list for special event notices and preferred customer status. To join, the website says you send an email with the subject line "Rock lobster." Whether you want to be on a mailing list is your call, but the instruction is on the site, so it is not a trick.

A few practical notes

  • If you want to take fish home and also eat there, the website's description suggests you can do both at one stop, since the market and the dining room share the address. Ask which entrance to use.
  • For a larger party, call ahead. The website does not describe a reservation policy.
  • If you have a food allergy, tell the kitchen. The website does not say how allergens are handled.
  • The site does not mention catering or takeout, so ask if you need either.
