Print My Plans is a plan-printing business at 97 Northampton Blvd in Toms River. Contractors, architects and designers upload a PDF, pick a sheet size and black and white or color, and the prints ship to a jobsite, office or home. The office is open Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET.

This is a profile of one business, not a ranking. The prices, sizes, hours and codes below are what printmyplans.com published on October 16, 2026. The account of the two partners comes from the owners.

Who runs it

The owners describe Print My Plans as a partnership between Eli Gewirtzman and Aryeh Kaplan. The about page on the website says the business is run by owner Eli and based at the Northampton Boulevard address.

Eli recently left a management role at a large printing and promotional products company. He has more than a decade in printing, focused on technical setup and how the work moves through the shop. He says he used that to build an automated workflow that cuts out human error and handles volume without a minimum order.

Aryeh is a general contractor in Central Jersey with eight years in construction. The owners say he is known for the details behind the walls and for finishing on time and on budget, and that his view of the jobsite is what keeps the shop aimed at what contractors actually need: ordering plans online instead of leaving a job to wait at a print counter.

What they print, and what it costs

The site prints from PDF, to scale, exactly as uploaded. Before you upload, the page size of the PDF has to match the sheet size you want. They print a single sheet, a full plan set, or several copies of a set. There is no minimum.

Every sheet is the same price, whatever the size. Black and white is $1.95 a page. Color is $3.75 a page. The site says color is meant for legends, markups and annotations, and black and white for line work. Sizes are 18×24 (ARCH C), 24×36 (ARCH D), 30×42 (ARCH E1) and 36×48 (ARCH E), each in black and white or color.

Prints are rolled and shipped in tubes. Every order gets tracking, and you can add a customer P.O. or reference number. The shipping rate shows at checkout. Orders in by 3 p.m. ET ship the same day, and the site says they can arrive as soon as the next day. Shipping is offered to a United States address (the site's shipping data lists the country as the US, with the origin ZIP 08757). If they make a mistake, the discounts page says they will reprint it and ship the reprint free.
