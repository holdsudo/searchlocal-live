Monday is the day many small businesses take off, which can make it hard to get an errand done at the start of the week. This post collects six businesses in the directory whose published hours include Monday.

They cover very different needs, from a pet appointment to a legal consultation. The hours below are as published in the directory. Schedules change, so call ahead before you go.

Spa Virtue, Toms River

Spa Virtue is a day spa at 1922 Hooper Ave, Suite 2D. The directory lists Cryoskin, Hydrafacial, Slim Wrap body wraps, massage and lash lifts among its services, plus infrared sauna sessions and spa packages.

The directory lists Monday hours of 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. No hours are listed for Tuesday or Sunday, so check before planning around those days. Phone: (732) 323-3000. See the directory listing or visit spavirtue.net.

Bridge Veterinary Hospital, Point Pleasant

Bridge Veterinary Hospital is a general-practice animal hospital at 2700 Bridge Avenue. The directory lists care for dogs, cats and exotic pets such as birds, rabbits and reptiles, with wellness exams, vaccinations, diagnostic imaging, lab work and an online pharmacy. It says it has served the Point Pleasant community since 2015.

Monday hours are listed as 8 a.m. to 7 p.m., the same as Tuesday through Friday. Saturday is listed as 8 a.m. to 3 p.m., and no Sunday hours are listed. Phone: (567) 361-3407. Website.

Tiny Teeth of Lakewood, Lakewood

Tiny Teeth of Lakewood is a children's dental office at 1000 Route 70 #8, led by pediatric dentist Dr. Zaki Mustacchi. The directory lists care for kids of all ages, including special-needs patients, with preventive and restorative dentistry, sedation dentistry and dental emergencies.

Monday hours are listed as 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Friday runs shorter, ending at 2:30 p.m., and no weekend hours are listed. Phone: (848) 245-0779. Website.

{{card:tiny-teeth-of-lakewood}}

Epstein's Meats, Lakewood

Epstein's Meats is a family-owned butcher shop at 391 Monmouth Avenue that says it has been serving customers since 1964. The directory lists custom-cut beef and other meats, prepared takeout food and catering orders, with delivery available on weekdays.

Monday hours are listed as 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Thursday runs until midnight, Friday is 8:30 a.m. to 2 p.m., and Sunday is 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. No Saturday hours are listed. Phone: (732) 370-3443. Website.

Brick Nails and Spa, Brick

Brick Nails and Spa is a nail salon at 56 Chambers Bridge Road, Suite 12. The directory lists manicures, pedicures, gel, acrylic and dip powder services, signature pedicures and waxing.

This is the only business here with hours listed every day of the week. Monday through Friday is 9:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m., Saturday is 9:30 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Sunday is 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Phone: (732) 920-9788. Website.

Hiering, Dupignac, Dunn & Schreiber, P.C., Toms River

This law firm has an office at 236 Washington Street. The directory lists estate planning, elder law, guardianships, land use and zoning, and workers' compensation among the areas it handles, for individuals, families and businesses across New Jersey.

Monday through Friday hours are listed as 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., with no weekend hours listed. Phone: (732) 349-1212. Website.

Planning your Monday

Hours vary a lot even among businesses that open on Mondays. Spa Virtue closes by 3 p.m., while the vet and the nail salon stay open into the evening. If you need a specific service, phone first and confirm both the hours and whether you need an appointment.
